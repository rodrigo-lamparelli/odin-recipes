Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese. It is a hearty and comforting meal that is perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. Heat the meat sauce in a large skillet over medium heat.
  4. Spread a thin layer of sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  5. Layer the cooked noodles, meat sauce, and cheese in the baking dish.
  6. Repeat the layers, ending with a layer of cheese on top.
  7. Bake for 30-40 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
