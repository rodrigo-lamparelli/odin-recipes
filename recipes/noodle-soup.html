Noodle Soup

Description

Noodle soup is a comforting and versatile dish that can be customized with various ingredients. It's perfect for a quick meal or when you're feeling under the weather.

Steps

  1. Bring water to a boil in a large pot.
  2. Add the noodles and cook according to the package instructions.
  3. In a separate pan, heat the broth and add your choice of vegetables and protein.
  4. Once the noodles are cooked, drain them and add them to the broth.
  5. Simmer for a few more minutes to allow the flavors to meld together.
