Tacos

Description

Tacos are a beloved Mexican dish consisting of a soft or hard shell filled with seasoned meat, vegetables, and various toppings.

Steps

  1. Season the ground beef with salt, pepper, and your favorite spices.
  2. Cook the seasoned beef in a skillet over medium heat until fully cooked.
  3. Warm the taco shells in the oven or microwave.
  4. Fill each shell with a portion of the cooked beef.
  5. Add your choice of toppings such as lettuce, cheese, tomatoes, and salsa.
